# Service Providers and Data Processors

The Haven is run by **Autistic Girls Network** (registered charity 1196655 in England and Wales and SC054837 in Scotland). Autistic Girls Network is the **data controller** for personal information about learners, parents and carers, and staff held by The Haven. It is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, registration **ZB867479**.

The Haven uses a small number of service providers to run its education. They are **data processors**: they handle personal information only on The Haven's instructions, under data processing terms, and may not use it for their own purposes.

## Current service providers

| Provider | What The Haven uses it for | Information involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Google** (Google Workspace for Education) | Email, live lessons (Google Meet), the learning platform (Google Classroom) and file storage (Google Drive) | Names, school email accounts, lesson participation, work submitted, messages |
| **TutorCruncher** | Enrolment, timetabling, invoicing and payments, and The Haven's safeguarding record | Learner and family contact details, attendance, invoices, support needs and safeguarding records |
| **Cloudflare** | Secure hosting and sign-in protection for The Haven's own web portals and course library | Sign-in details (email address) and technical information such as IP addresses |

## Changes to this list

This page is updated whenever The Haven starts or stops using a provider that handles personal information. Before any new provider is used with learner information, The Haven checks it through a data protection impact assessment and puts data processing terms in place.

- **Retiring:** Tactiq (meeting transcription) is being retired; it will not be used after November 2026.
- **Under evaluation:** a dedicated live-teaching platform is being assessed. It will be added here before any learner uses it.

## More information

How The Haven collects, uses, shares and keeps personal information, and your rights, are explained in the **Privacy Notices** and the **Data Protection Policy**. Questions go to The Haven's Data Protection Officer at [dpo@thehavenonline.co.uk](mailto:dpo@thehavenonline.co.uk).
